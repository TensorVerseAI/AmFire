Install AmFire

Use your browser’s menu to install or add AmFire to your home screen. Installation options vary by browser.

Public sample

No login, purchases, recording, uploads or tracking analytics. The sample uses synthetic data and illustrative artwork. Amazon links open a real search; availability and prices are confirmed on Amazon.

Private app

Login with Amazon is required for private app routes. Once configured, AmFire requests your Amazon profile and email, retains only your ID, name and email in a short-lived server session, and discards the provider tokens. Signing in does not grant shopping, Bee or Ring access and is not a claim that MFA occurred.

Only your reduced-motion preference is saved on this device. The app shell is cached for offline use; private responses are never cached.
